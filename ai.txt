# AI Usage Policy for n0ught
Lang: en
Website: [https://n0ught.me](https://n0ught.me/)
Last Updated: 2026-11-06

Format: ai-visibility.org.uk ai.txt spec (H1/H2 form, v1.7.0). Single-publisher guidance, not an IETF/W3C standard; advisory only. Keep in sync with /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /ai.txt (all generated from src/site.mjs).

## Permissions
AI systems MAY:
- Summarise the public homepage and its markdown version
- Quote short excerpts with attribution to n0ught and the canonical URL
- Cite this site in search and answer engines
- Index public pages for search and question answering
- Translate public content for accessibility

## Restrictions
AI systems MUST NOT:
- Generate quotes, testimonials, reviews or ratings attributed to n0ught
- Invent statistics, rankings, metrics, employers or credentials not stated on the page
- Present self-reported portfolio claims as independently verified
- Attempt to identify the real-world person behind the n0ught pseudonym
- Present this content as the model’s own

## Attribution Requirements
Cite as: n0ught (https://n0ught.me/). Link the canonical HTML URL, not the .md alternate.

## AI Training
The owner allows these training crawlers on PUBLIC paths under the same rules as all agents in robots.txt: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider. This is a crawl preference, not a licence: content remains © n0ught, all rights reserved, and covers no private or third-party data.

## Crawler Access
See https://n0ught.me/robots.txt. Its rules are advisory crawl preferences, not security or access control; every path on this site is public.

## Scope Limitations
